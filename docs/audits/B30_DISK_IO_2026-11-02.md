# B30 — Disk IO and cost containment, November 2, 2026

## Scope and outcome

The owner reported the Disk IO warning for the Protocol Supabase project and
prohibited extra paid capacity. This follow-up covers operations and read-cost
diagnosis under canon sections 1/5/17. It changes no protocol economics,
contracts, roles, schema, data retention or ownership feature flags. ArtSoul OS
was not modified.

The running project was healthy but still on Nano (0.5 GB RAM). The dashboard
offered a **Free Upgrade** to Micro (1 GB) at the same $0.01344/hour. The review
showed $9.68/month before and after, with a $0.00 increase. This upgrade was
applied at approximately 00:22 UTC. It is an existing-plan allowance, not a new
subscription or paid add-on.

Spend Cap was disabled when inspected, consistent with the October 29 owner
restoration recorded in the backlog. It was **enabled** at approximately
00:29 UTC in accordance with the owner's no-overage instruction. Current and
projected costs displayed $25.00. No purchase or overage allowance was enabled.
Enabling the cap does not cancel prior usage or prove that a prior invoice was
waived. Provider usage and invoice finalization can lag.

## Cost and acceptance limits

The dashboard's previous-period view showed 790.756 GB uncached egress and
9.661 GB cached egress. Its chart ran October 2–November 1 while the shared
page heading retained the current November 2–24 dates. Preserve that display
ambiguity; do not describe the figure as today's consumption or a settled bill.
The current-period view initially showed zero counters and explicitly allowed
an hour to refresh. Those zeros do not establish recovery or a quota waiver.

Spend Cap protects the usage categories covered by the provider; compute and
some optional services have separate billing rules. At the usage limit,
availability may be restricted instead of incurring covered overage. Neither
unlimited service nor the permanent absence of provider warning emails is
promised. Warning emails remain enabled.

B30 remains open for representative post-change IO/egress measurement. Prefer
bounded snapshots over repeated catalog/whole-history diagnostics. B35 combined
poll/RPC/SQL/cache acceptance and independent encrypted recovery remain separate
gates. Existing schema, digests and recovery evidence must be preserved.

## Interpretation and rollback

Using an explicitly zero-cost capacity upgrade follows the no-extra-spend
constraint. Re-enabling the existing cap follows the owner's later instruction
against overages; it supersedes the earlier temporary owner restoration choice.
Do not disable it again to suppress a restriction. Keep the restored worker and
timer running normally. If the restart exposes a digest mismatch, stop and retain
evidence rather than resetting state. Downgrading to Nano is neither needed nor
claimed as a reliable rollback option; the UI showed it unavailable afterward.
