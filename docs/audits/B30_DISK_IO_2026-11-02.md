prohibited extra paid capacity. This follow-up covers operations and read-cost
diagnosis under canon sections 1/5/17. It changes no protocol economics,

offered a **Free Upgrade** to Micro (1 GB) at the same $0.01344/hour. The review

subscription or paid add-on.

restoration recorded in the backlog. It was **enabled** at approximately

projected costs displayed $25.00. No purchase or overage allowance was enabled.
Enabling the cap does not cancel prior usage or prove that a prior invoice was
waived. Provider usage and invoice finalization can lag.

constraint. Re-enabling the existing cap follows the owner's later instruction
against overages; it supersedes the earlier temporary owner restoration choice.
Do not disable it again to suppress a restriction. Keep the restored worker and
timer running normally. If the restart exposes a digest mismatch, stop and retain
evidence rather than resetting state. Downgrading to Nano is neither needed nor
claimed as a reliable rollback option; the UI showed it unavailable afterward.
