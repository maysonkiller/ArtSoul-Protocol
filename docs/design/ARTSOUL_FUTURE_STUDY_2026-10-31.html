What is a wallet?

A wallet holds your blockchain account and approves actions. ArtSoul should never ask you to type its recovery phrase into the website.

What does a verification mark mean? Proposed mark

In this proposed interface it means the connection between a creator and a collection has been reviewed. It does not guarantee investment value, copyright ownership or safety. The issuing workflow is not active in this demo.

Can I change my mind?

Saved items can be removed. A listing can be withdrawn by its seller when permitted. Submitted blockchain transactions cannot simply be undone. Each action must explain its own effect before approval.
