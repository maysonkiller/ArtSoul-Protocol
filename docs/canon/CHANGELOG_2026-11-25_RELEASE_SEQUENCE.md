# November 25 release decisions

## Testnet rehearsal and production custody

Use fresh instances of the replacement auction engine and its paired NFT in Base Sepolia. Reuse the existing test wallets where their address, network, custody role and current access can be established. Keep the old deployment and its data available. Confirm the new pair, both directions of the binding, all constructor values and the deployment receipt before routing public writes to it. Choose and verify the new production wallets separately before any Base mainnet deployment. A test receipt authorizes no production operation.

## Security and operating sequence

Phase C covers internal review of the exact release candidate, finding disposition, reproducible build evidence and the fresh testnet rehearsal. The full independent audit moves to the end of Phase D. Record its scope, findings, repairs and retest before D closes. Before exposing real funds, the owner must review outstanding security risks and approve the specific release and its current audit status. Keep an unresolved issue that could affect funds or withdrawal access visible in that decision.

Business incorporation is deferred while development and the testnet rehearsal continue. Before charging real fees, identify the lawful operator and obligations that apply to the actual service, including accounting, user terms, privacy and complaints. Record protocol fees separately from sale volume, seller and artist proceeds, refundable deposits and the ecosystem treasury. The first actual revenue triggers a business-structure review. Any obligation arising earlier applies at its own effective date.

The founder will decide whether to seek investment after observing demand on a working product. Use confirmed purchases by distinct collectors, returning participants, fees actually earned and operating costs. Exclude rehearsal transactions from that assessment. No revenue target or minimum observation period has been selected. If the service closes, preserve withdrawal, refund and NFT access, publish the applicable contract addresses and retain recovery information.

## Networks and Genesis

Base Sepolia remains the write rehearsal. Future Robinhood issuance covers ordinary artworks and collections. It needs network-specific deployments, authority and indexing. Arc-origin payments for Base assets are a separate future integration. Payment evidence must identify the actual destination settlement before ArtSoul records a paid sale or issues an NFT.

Genesis on-chain activity eligibility uses Base mainnet. The founder retired the public testnet Snapshot A and its advance announcement. Normal backups, verified restore and data preservation still apply. This decision authorizes neither a database reset nor production activation.
